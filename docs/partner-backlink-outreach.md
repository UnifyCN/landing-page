# Partner backlink outreach

Off-page SEO for unifysocial.ca. The 17 organisations in `src/lib/partners.ts` each have a
detail page on the site (`/partners/[slug]`). A link back from their site is the single
biggest lever for the head terms ("newcomers in Canada", "newcomer app") that content alone
cannot win against government domains.

Send from a personal address, one org at a time. Ask for one specific page, with a suggested
anchor. Libraries, neighbourhood houses, YMCA, and United Way all keep "newcomer resources"
pages that list third-party tools; that is the target, not the homepage.

## Email template

**Subject:** A newcomer resource for the [ORG] website

Hi [FIRST NAME],

Thanks again for partnering with Unify on [EVENT / PROGRAM]. [ONE SENTENCE OF SPECIFIC RESULT, e.g. "Forty-two newcomers came through the Burnaby session in August."]

One small ask. Would you add Unify to the newcomer resources page on [ORG] site? It is free for
newcomers, built in Vancouver, and it covers the first-month tasks your staff answer questions
about every day: health card, SIN, bank account, driver's licence, and finding work.

Suggested listing:

> **Unify - newcomer settlement app for Canada.** Free checklists, plain-language guides, and a
> community of newcomers. https://unifysocial.ca

If it is easier, a link to one of our guides works too. Three that your visitors ask about:

- Health card waiting period by province: https://unifysocial.ca/health-card
- Exchanging a foreign driver's licence: https://unifysocial.ca/drivers-licence
- TEER categories and PR eligibility: https://unifysocial.ca/teer

Happy to return the favour on our partner page, which already lists [ORG]:
https://unifysocial.ca/partners/[SLUG]

Thanks,
Savar
